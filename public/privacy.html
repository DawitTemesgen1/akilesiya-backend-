Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 16, 2026

Welcome to አቅሌስያ (Akilesiya). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you register for an account or use our features:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number (primary and secondary), date of birth, gender, and religious affiliation details (Christian name, confession father).
  • User Content: Photos (profile pictures), posts, comments, and other content you upload to the app.
  • Technical Data: We may collect basic information about your device to improve app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your profile in the Sunday School system.
  • Communication: To send you notifications about Sunday School activities and updates.
  • Personalization: To tailor your experience within the app based on your role and Sunday School affiliation.
  • Safety and Security: To verify users and protect the integrity of our community.

3. Data Sharing

Your data is shared within the "አቅሌስያ" platform in the following ways:

  • Community Sharing: Posts and comments you create are visible to other members of your Sunday School.
  • Admin Access: Authorized Sunday School administrators have access to your profile data for attendance and management purposes.
  • Third Parties: We do not sell your personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact your Sunday School administrator or visit our data removal portal.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/SSL) to protect your data during transmission between the app and our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact your Sunday School administrator or email us through our support channels.
